# Labyrinth game

A keyboard game for one 5-year-old girl. The Child moves a character through Labyrinths to reach the Goal.

## Language

**Labyrinth**:
One grid of paths with branches and dead ends, from a start square to a Goal.
_Avoid_: maze, map, board, level

**Goal**:
The square in a Labyrinth that the Child must reach.
_Avoid_: exit, finish, end

**Item**:
A small thing on the path that the Child can collect, for example a shell or a fish. The chest on the Goal has a padlock until the Child collects all Items of the Labyrinth, so she must collect all Items to finish a Labyrinth.
_Avoid_: pickup, coin, treasure, collectible

**Sea friend**:
An animal that the Child gets when she reaches the Goal.
_Avoid_: reward, sticker, prize

**Collection**:
All the Sea friends that the Child has, and the screen that shows them.
_Avoid_: album, inventory, sticker book

**Area**:
A part of the sea with its own look, that holds a group of Labyrinths, for example a lagoon or a coral reef.
_Avoid_: world, zone, chapter, stage

**Voyage**:
The 20 Labyrinths from Labyrinth 1 to the Goal of Labyrinth 20. Each Voyage has its own Labyrinths.
_Avoid_: round, run, game, level

**Opening screen**:
The screen with the logo and the start button.
_Avoid_: title screen, menu, home

**Party**:
The end screen of a Voyage.
_Avoid_: credits, victory screen

**Child**:
The one player of the game: the Parent's 5-year-old daughter, who cannot read but knows numbers well.
_Avoid_: player, user, kid

**Parent**:
The adult who starts the game and controls it outside of play.
_Avoid_: admin, user
