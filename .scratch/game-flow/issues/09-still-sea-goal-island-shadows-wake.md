# 09: Still sea, Goal island, shadows and wake

**What to build:** A still background with a soft foam edge, a larger chest on its own 2-tile island with a palm, static shadows under the boat and the Items, and a soft foam wake. Spec: [spec.md](../spec.md), Part 5, fixes 2, 4, 8 (shadows and light) and 9. Mockup: [polish-MOCKUP](../prototypes/polish-MOCKUP/index.html).

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** resolved

- [x] Water: the light patterns go away. The wave lines stay but do not move. Nothing in the background moves.
- [x] Foam: a still soft edge along each coast (a white line of 6 px at 35 % with soft edges, under the islands) replaces the moving dashed foam. It uses the coast lines that the game already computes.
- [x] The islands keep all their Kenney details: sand, grass dots, rocks and bushes.
- [x] Goal island: the tile right of the Goal island tile, outside the Labyrinth, becomes island too. The Goal island is 2 tiles wide, with a sand coast all around, and no rock and no bush on these 2 tiles.
- [x] The chest sits on the seam of the 2 tiles, 115 % of a tile wide, fully on land. The palm sits on the outer tile, up and to the right of the chest, inside the coast.
- [x] Shadows: the boat and each Item on the water get a static ellipse shadow (80 % of the sprite wide, 22 % high, black at 20 %, 28 % of the sprite below its centre and a little to the right). The boat shadow moves with the boat. No shadow in the Item column or in the strip.
- [x] Wake: soft white dots behind the stern replace the ellipse rings (radius from 9 % of a tile, smaller and fainter with age, start opacity 42 %, life 0.6 s, a new dot every 40 ms while the boat moves). The dots stay under the islands.
- [x] Tests check what the Child can see: for example no moving water parameter, the chest fully on land in all 20 Labyrinths, and the wake dots under the islands.
- [x] The play shots of all 4 Areas are taken again.
- [x] The build spec (`.scratch/labyrinth-game/spec.md`) gets errata for each section that this ticket changes.

Build note (gf/09-still-sea-goal-island): The water shader has no light patterns and no time parameter. Each wave line keeps the place of the old wave at t = 0, `8 * sin(0.02 x + 0.05 y)` px from its grid place, so the lines are not in a strict grid. WaterView, FoamView and AreaDecoration have no `_process()`. The Area decoration stays and does not move: crests at 85 %, 3 bubbles at 70 %, 2 drops at 80 %, 12 stars each with its own alpha from 0.2 to 0.9 (the build orchestrator approved this scope change). The palm still sways (the build orchestrator kept it). The foam is a Line2D on each coast loop with a 1 × 32 px soft edge texture: 6 px wide, white at 35 %, the alpha goes up from 0 in the outer 35 % of each half of the width. Its centre is 3 px from the coast, so the line starts at the sand. `src/foam.gdshader` is gone. `CoastLine.outer_outline()` adds the outer Goal island tile to the outer coast, so the foam goes round the Goal island. `IslandLook.goal_island_tiles()` gives the 2 Goal island tiles. The outer tile cells come after the Labyrinth cells, so the tile variants and the decorations of the Labyrinth tiles do not change, and the Python port still passes. The chest is 1.15 tile wide, with its centre on the seam and on the middle of the row. The palm is 0.6 tile (it was 0.7), at (1.66, 0.32) tiles from the top-left corner of the Goal island tile. At 0.7 tile the palm does not fit inside the coast of the outer tile. A test samples every 4th pixel of the chest and the palm pictures (the palm at -0.08, 0 and +0.08 rad) and checks the Kenney tile pixel below: 0 pixels are off land in Labyrinths 1 to 20. A test with the chest 20 px higher finds 408 pixels off land, so the check works. The shadow is the new class `FloatShadow`: the sprite size is its longer side (110 px for the boat, 90 px for an Item), and "a little to the right" is 5 % of the sprite size. The boat shadow keeps the screen axes, so it does not turn with the boat, and it does not rock, bob or hop. The wake dots start 58 px behind the boat centre (the stern is at 55 px), 4 px to the left and right in turn, and shrink to 40 % of the start radius. The dots use a radial soft texture, full white to half the radius. `Motion.FRIEND_START_PX` is (175, 60) in place of (60, 60): at +60 the Sea friend covered 0.53 to 0.73 of the larger chest. This value is temporary until ticket 11 moves the reveal to the screen centre. Until ticket 08 lands, the outer Goal island tile is outside the Labyrinth rectangle of the layout, and the Sea friend grows up to 0.4 tile right of the outer tile. Shots (`shots/area1.png` to `area4.png`, `area1-1920x1247.png`): in all 4 Areas the chest is fully on land, the palm is inside the coast up and to the right of the chest, the boat is inside its channel, and the foam edge is soft and still. A person must check: the palm is about half behind the chest; the boat shadow is wider than the boat when the boat points down or up, and it is faint on the dark water of Area 4; an Item shadow shows mostly below the Item.
