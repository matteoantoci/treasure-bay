# 08: Screen layout and HUD

**What to build:** A larger maze with the Item column at its left, clear Item slots, a Collection strip with 20 slots and the total count in the rounded font, a quiet gear, and the settings panel in the cream-and-wood style. Spec: [spec.md](../spec.md), Part 5, fixes 1, 3, 6 and 7, and the outline rule of fix 8. Mockup: [polish-MOCKUP](../prototypes/polish-MOCKUP/index.html) and its real-layout shots.

**Blocked by:** 05 (Name and opening screen; it adds the font)

**Status:** resolved

- [x] The top band goes away. All HUD values use the HUD scale h = visible height / 760.
- [x] The Item column is 100 h wide at the left, with a margin of 12 h. The Labyrinth is centred in the space right of the Item column and above the strip, less the margins.
- [x] The tile is the largest that fits, with a cap of 121 × visible height / 1200. The Labyrinth width counts one tile more for the Goal island (ticket 09).
- [x] The boat stays 85 % of a tile long, and an Item stays 69 % of a tile.
- [x] Item slots: a disc with fill white at 22 % and an outline of 3 px in white at 85 %. The outline contrast against the water is 3:1 or more in all 4 Areas. The diameter is min(120 px, (free height − (count − 1) × 16 px) / count) at 1200 px. The group is centred. An empty slot shows the grey Item at 35 %. A collected Item fills its slot with the bounce.
- [x] Collection strip: same height, 20 slots (fill white at 14 %, outline 2 px in white at 45 %), a gap of 12 px or more, and 24 px or more from the screen edges. The total count is at the left in the rounded font with an outline and a spiral shell icon.
- [x] Gear: 48 px, 40 % opacity, its centre 40 px from the top and the right edges. It does not overlap the Labyrinth.
- [x] Settings panel: black at 50 % behind it, fill `#fff3d6`, border 10 px `#7a4a24`, corner radius 40 px, buttons `#e9c98f` with a bottom edge `#b58a4a`, icons `#5a3416`. The volume bars, the hold rings, the reset ring colour and the hold times stay.
- [x] Screen layout tests at 1920 × 1200 and at 1512 × 982: the tile cap, the Item column, the slot gaps (16 px in the column with 8 Items, 12 px in the strip, 24 px from the edges), and the gear outside the Labyrinth.
- [x] The build spec (`.scratch/labyrinth-game/spec.md`) gets errata for each section that this ticket changes.

Build note (gf/08-screen-layout-and-hud): `ScreenLayout` has no top band. `labyrinth_area()` is the space right of the Item column (100 h) and above the strip (70 h), less 12 h on each side: 1724.2 × 1051.1 px from (176.8, 18.9) at 1920 × 1200. It stays the one source of the area: the reveal of ticket 11 uses its middle, now (1038.9, 544.5) at 1200. The px values of the HUD are px at a visible height of 1200 px and scale with the visible height (`ScreenLayout.scaled_px()`), so the HUD keeps its proportions on the 1512 × 982 screen (visible 1920 × 1247). `tile_px()` is `floor(min(area height / rows, area width / (columns + 1), 121 × Hv / 1200))`. `labyrinth_rect()` is still the rectangle of the Labyrinth tiles, so the views do not change. The Labyrinth plus the outer Goal island tile at its right are centred in the area, on whole px. Tile px at 1920 × 1200 (at 1920 × 1247): 4 × 3 and 5 × 3 121 (125), 5 × 4 and 6 × 4 116 (121), 7 × 4 107 (107), 7 × 5 and 8 × 5 95 (99 and 95), 8 × 6 and 9 × 6 80 (84), 9 × 7 and 10 × 7 70 (72). Labyrinth 18 goes from 65 px to 80 px, Labyrinth 20 from 56 px to 70 px.

Item slots: `ItemRow` keeps its name and its `outlines` (grey sprites) and `fills` arrays. Each slot has a new `ItemRow.SlotDisc` under the sprites. `outline_centre()` is now `slot_centre()`. The Item sprite is 70 % of the disc diameter (the mockup value; the spec gives none). The disc pulses with the sprites at a visit to the locked chest (ticket 06), and it does not bounce when the Item arrives. Difference from the spec: white at 85 % on the water has only 2.12:1 contrast in Area 1 and 2.56:1 in Area 2 (pure white gives only 2.41:1 and 2.97:1), so the spec outline alone cannot reach 3:1. Beat-pals rule C7 asks for 3:1 against the colours next to the outline, so the disc has a dark edge of 1.5 px of `#0a1428` at 60 % on its rim, outside the 3 px white outline (approved by the build orchestrator). The edge and the outline are inside the disc diameter, so the gap stays 16 px. The disc draws 0.5 px inside its radius, because the antialiased rim reaches about half a px outside.

Collection strip: the shell icon is 58 px, 24 px from the left edge, and the count is right of it, left aligned, in `GameFont.bold()` at 64 px with the outline of before. The count area ends at 300 px. The 20 discs are `min(68 px, (width − 19 × 12 px) / 20)` from 300 px to 24 px from the right edge: 68 px with a gap of 12.42 px at 1200, 67.3 px with a gap of 12.47 px at 1247. A Sea friend is 90 % of its disc. The 68 px, the 300 px, the 58 px and the 90 % come from the mockup. Outline widths: fix 8 says "one outline width for HUD discs", but fix 3 gives 3 px and fix 6 gives 2 px. The build keeps 3 px for the Item slots and 2 px for the strip slots, as the explicit values say (build orchestrator decision).

Gear: 48 px at 40 % opacity, a white gear with a hole and no dark outline, as the mockup. It is two half polygons, so no part draws two times at 40 %. At 1200 its rectangle is (1856, 16, 48, 48).

Settings panel: a `ColorRect` of black at 50 % (`dim`) under the panel. It takes no clicks, so the gear still closes the panel. Panel padding 40 px top and bottom, 56 px at the sides, rows 28 px apart (mockup). Minus and plus are round buttons of 72 px with a bottom edge of 6 px; the face moves down onto the edge while a button is down. Pictures are flat, 56 px, `#5a3416`. Level bars are the mockup bars: 34 px wide, 24, 36, 50 and 64 px tall, `#5a3416` full and `#d8b98a` empty. The hold rings are 120 px and 14 px thick, empty in `#e9c98f`, with a cream disc inside. Difference from the spec: the quit ring fills in `#5a3416`, not `#ffd447`, because yellow has 1.12:1 contrast on the `#e9c98f` ring. The reset ring stays `#ff7a59`, and the hold times stay 8 s and 3 s.
