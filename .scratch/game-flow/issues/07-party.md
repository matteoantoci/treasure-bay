Build note (gf/07-party): The new node `Party` (`src/party_view.gd`, `PartyView`) sits in `main.tscn` after `Opening` and before `Gear`, so the gear stays above it and the Fade covers it. The main scene keeps the Goal celebration of Labyrinth 20 as it is up to the flamingo in its slot (3.7 s). At 4.2 s it emits `party_coming` in place of `next_labyrinth_coming` and fades to dark. At 4.7 s it shows the still Party picture in place of the next Labyrinth and fades in. At 5.2 s (the end of the fade-in) the Party timeline starts and `party_started` fires. So the timeline of spec part 4 starts at the end of the fade-in, and the Child sees each pop. A press after the first 3.0 s fades to dark in 500 ms, then the opening screen shows and fades in in 500 ms. The Collection strip hides on the Party, as on the opening screen. `show_party()` shows the Party at once and starts it, for tests and the shot tool. The picture uses the prototype values times 1.2 (1600 × 1000 to 1920 × 1200) and scales with `min(Hv / 1200, Wv / 1920)`, so the ring of 1680 px fits on a 4:3 screen too. Island: `IslandLook.block(4 × 3)` of 150 px tiles, through the new `OpeningScreen.new_island()` that the opening screen now uses too. Chest 228 px wide. Boat 180 px long with its bow up, and it rocks. Ring radii (780, 480) px, Sea friends 120 px. Star 228 px, number 73 px. Values that the spec does not give: the pop of the prototype `popin` (0 to 1.25 in 350 ms `out`, then 1 in 150 ms); the 8 Item kinds and their arc of the prototype; the Item flight of 0.7 s with `back`; the confetti of the build spec (40 pieces, not the 70 of the prototype) for the chest burst and the loop bursts, scaled by `h`; the burst place in x −600 to +600 px and y 240 to 720 px. The wave height is 40 px of the HUD scale (`h`), as the spec says, so 63 px at 1200, not the 48 px of the prototype. Sound: the music crossfades to Area 1 at the fade to the Party (the spec gives no Party music). Each Sea friend sound file has its own player (polyphony 4) in `Sound.play_party_friend()`, so the 20 sounds overlap, as in the prototype. The chest plays `goal.wav`. The Party loads its 20 Sea friend pictures at its first show, not at launch: a load at launch made the first frame after a launch about 90 ms longer, and the opening screen test of the 140 ms button press failed. One frame moves the Party time by at most 1/30 s (times the time scale), so the slow frame of that load does not skip the first pops. A person must check `shots/party.png` against the prototype, round 1, variant B, and the sound of 20 overlapping Sea friend sounds on the Mac.
