## Question

Does the game stop or slow down after some time, to limit screen time?

Recommendation: no limit in the game. The Parent controls the time. A 5-year-old does not understand a limit that comes from the game, and the game cannot explain it without words.

## Decision

The Parent accepted the recommendation on 2026-11-03.
