- **One place for the values:** `src/motion.gd` (`Motion`, `RefCounted`) holds every value of spec 8 and the boat timings of spec 5.2 and 5.3 (moved out of `MoveRule`), the curves (`Motion.Easing` OUT, INOUT, BACK; `Motion.shape(tweener, curve)`), the pure idle, water, wake, and decoration functions, `Motion.goal_steps()` (the Goal celebration as [seconds, step]: chest 0, hops 0 and 0.6, confetti 0, Sea friend 1.4, wiggle 1.9, fly 3.0, slot 3.7, fade out 4.2, next Labyrinth 4.7, unlock 5.2), and `Motion.starts_new_area(n)`.
- **Idle and water:** `BoatView` rocks and bobs while `idle` (off while it moves and from the Goal to the unlock) and has `hop()`. `ItemsView` bobs each Item and draws its sparkle. `LabyrinthView` sways the palm and has `open_chest()` (each new Labyrinth shows the closed chest). `WaterView` moves the waves and the light shapes (new shader uniforms `wave_phase`, `light_shift`). `AreaDecoration` moves its pieces (crests drift on an 80 px path and fade in and out, bubbles rise, drops fall, stars twinkle). New `FoamView` + `src/foam.gdshader` + `CoastLine` (pure): a dashed Line2D along the coast of the inner water and around the outer coast, rounded at the island corners like the Kenney corner tiles.
