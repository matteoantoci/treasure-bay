# Reward after Labyrinth 20

Type: decision
Status: resolved
Blocked by: none
Map: [Labyrinth game for a 5-year-old](../map.md)

## Question

After Labyrinth 20, all 20 Sea friends are in the Collection, but random Labyrinths continue. What does the Goal show then? Which Area look do the random Labyrinths use?

Recommendation: at the Goal, one Sea friend from the Collection comes back and does the arrival motion again. The game picks it at random, but not the same friend two times in a row. The random Labyrinths change the Area every 5 Labyrinths, in the same order as Labyrinths 1 to 20.

## Decision

The Parent accepted the recommendation on 2026-11-03.
